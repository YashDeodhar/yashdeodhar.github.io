About Me

I'm currently a PhD student in the Applied Math department at the University of California, Merced. Previously, I completed my B.tech with honors at Manipal Institute of Technology and then my master's in applied math at Northwestern University.

My primary research interest lies in finding efficient methods to solve PDEs - either through algorithms that leverage smart approximations or through efficient parallelization of the computational task.
