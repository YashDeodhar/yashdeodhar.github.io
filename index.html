Stability of Explicit Midpoint Rule

An example of theory versus practice. In theory, the region of absolute stability of the leapfrog method for this problem is the imaginary-axis segment (-i,i). Thus, for a nonzero real eigenvalue, no positive timestep is absolutely stable. In practice, however, for a finite final time T (e.g. T = 1 here), a sufficiently small timestep can keep the numerical solution bounded over [0,T], even though the solution eventually blows up.
